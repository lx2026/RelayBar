# App Store text

**Name** — RelayBar

**Subtitle** — Read files from your SSH host

**Description**

Read documents on your SSH host from your iPhone or iPad. Open a Markdown file with its relative images, jump to a heading or switch to the source. Continue Reading takes you back to your place.

Browse folders, inspect image details and read text or formatted JSON. Preview notebooks and tables, play video and keep favourites on Home. On iPad, read two pages side by side. Recently opened files can stay available while the host is out of reach.

Open a web dashboard by entering its port. RelayBar can reach services listening on the SSH host’s loopback address and asks before opening one on exact localhost.

Connect to a host you can reach, including one on your VPN. Check its SSH fingerprint on first use. Sign in with tailnet identity on a compatible server, a device key or a password.

**Keywords** — ssh,sftp,markdown,reader,remote,files,folders,tailnet,json,images

## What to test in TestFlight

Connect to a test host and compare its fingerprint. Open a document with a relative image, scroll to a heading and reopen it from Continue Reading. Switch away, interrupt the connection and return. Check the cached page, then reconnect and open a changed file. Include the device, OS version and the step where the result differed. Omit private paths and connection details.

## App Store privacy answers

- Do the developer or third-party partners collect data from the app? No. Select “Data Not Collected”.
- Which data types are linked to the user? None.
- Which data types are used for tracking? None.

Profiles, reading history and cached files stay on the device. Reading a file connects the device to the SSH host the user chose. RelayBar has no developer account system, analytics or advertising.
