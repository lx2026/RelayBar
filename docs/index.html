Paste an ssh command once. After that it is a switch: local, remote and SOCKS forwards over one managed connection, using the SSH already on your Mac.

It runs your own SSH

RelayBar starts /usr/bin/ssh for you. Your config, keys, known hosts and agent are used as they are.

It says what went wrong

Each tunnel shows its ports and its state. A dropped one retries at a pace you set, and a failed one shows the error.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No account, no analytics and no sync. Profiles and recent paths are stored locally.

Remote Files

A window onto a folder on your server. It connects only when you open a location.

  • Reopen a recent folder in one click, or add an exact path.
  • Preview images, video, Markdown and JSON in place.
  • Upload one file safely, download, or delete with five seconds to undo.

New in the 2.0 beta

Frame, a reader for what is in the folder

Images, video, Markdown and HTML, on your Mac or, through RelayBar, on your server. Nothing is imported.
